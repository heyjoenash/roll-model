# Prototype Sandbox

This is a development sandbox showing the early 3D asset prototypes running in one scene. The ball in the foreground is the existing Rev Rate asset. The pin deck behind it is built with drei's `Lathe` geometry using a USBC-proportioned profile — 10 pins in a proper equilateral triangle at 12-inch spacing.

Use the Leva panel to explore pin states, or click the buttons below to see interaction patterns that will power future content sections.

</Callout>

## What the ball touches

On a perfect strike, the ball only directly contacts 4 of the 10 pins. The other 6 fall from pin-to-pin collisions — the chain reaction is where most of the action happens. This toggle highlights the 4 pins the ball actually touches.

## Row by row

The 10-pin arrangement is an equilateral triangle in 4 rows — 1, 2, 3, 4 pins respectively. Pin 1 is the head pin; 7 and 10 are the corner pins in the back row.

## What's next

The pin asset is now a swappable module at `src/components/3d/assets/pins/`. When a high-end 3D team delivers proper pin models, only the index file changes — every scene that imports from `@/components/3d/assets/pins` gets upgraded automatically. Same pattern will apply to the Lane, Figure, and Ball assets.
